# Patterns

Patterns document recurring layouts and interaction compositions extracted from Patients and MPF Portal screens. They are documentation of existing product UI — not new components. Chrome patterns compose Templates plus Components: **Patients intake chrome** uses MultiStepFlowLayout (a Nuclear template, not a Patients layout); **Operational app chrome** uses AppShell.

Canonical documentation lives at `/docs/patterns`.

## Documented patterns

Storybook CSF exists for every documented recipe: Form field, Worklist table, Scan search, Workspace tabs, Operational app chrome, Patients step, Exclusive choice, Multi-select choice, In-step notice, Conditional reveal, Optional skip, Follow-up details, Document upload, and Patients intake chrome.
