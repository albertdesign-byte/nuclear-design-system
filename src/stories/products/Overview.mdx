# Products

Products consume Nuclear layouts. They do not own them.

AppShell and MultiStepFlowLayout live under **Templates/**. Domain copy, validation, routing, business rules, and sample data live here.

## Patients

Patient intake, registration, payment, insurance, consent, scheduling, and screening. Intake screens compose **MultiStepFlowLayout**.

## Providers, Clinics, and other domains

Future domain implementations (Provider Onboarding, Clinic Registration, Radiology Workflow, Insurance Enrollment) compose the same Nuclear templates. Do not create a product-named layout.

## MPF Portal

Operational study coordination, dashboard, search, and detail implementations. Existing `nuclear` route slugs remain in use for compatibility. Operational screens compose **AppShell**.
